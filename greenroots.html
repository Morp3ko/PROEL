Growing community, one garden at a time

GreenRoots partners with neighbourhoods to plant trees, tend shared gardens, and teach the next generation how to care for the land around them.

Rooted in the neighbourhoods we serve

Since 2011, GreenRoots has worked side by side with residents to turn vacant lots into gardens, line streets with shade trees, and give young people hands-on experience caring for green spaces.

Every project starts with the people who live there — we bring the tools, training, and native plants; the community brings the vision.

Where your time and support go

Three ongoing programs, each shaped by the neighbourhoods that host them.

Tree Planting

Community plantings that add shade and cool city blocks one street at a time, using species suited to the local climate.

Community Gardens

Shared plots where neighbours grow their own food and swap seeds, tools, and know-how across generations.

Youth Education

After-school and summer programs that teach kids to grow, harvest, and cook food from their own garden beds.
